Estimates notice

A tool to help you organise

Autonomy and calculations for water, food, pets, energy, consumption and restocking are indicative. They depend on your entries, units, configured needs and available history. A figure does not guarantee that supplies are sufficient, safe or suitable.

Data and limitations

Check quantities, expiry dates and the actual condition of products and equipment. Statistics reflect recorded movements only. Missing information can make estimates partial or prevent calculation. Wh expresses recorded energy capacity, not days or guaranteed performance.

Safety decisions

HomeRunway does not replace official civil protection or food safety guidance, or medical, nutritional, veterinary or technical advice. In an emergency, follow the competent authorities and professionals. Do not consume expired or unsafe products based on an app estimate.
