Privacy policy

Controller and contact

HomeRunway is a free household app. Owner and controller: Gonzalo Peralta Gaudes · inventsweneed@gmail.com. This policy covers the app and its information website.

Data and purposes

The information you enter includes household, member and pet names, their types and configured needs, locations, products, batches, quantities, expiry dates, consumption, targets, equipment, maintenance, ratings, comments and optional photos. It is used to organise supplies, calculate estimates, show statistics and schedule reminders. You do not need a separate HomeRunway account. Do not add medical or other sensitive information; use aliases where sufficient.

The app includes no advertising, tracking or external analytics. Statistics are calculated from household movements; they are not telemetry sent to the owner.

Device and iCloud

Records are stored on your device using SwiftData. Preferences and reminders are specific to each device. System backups may retain data according to your Apple settings.

iCloud is optional. When enabled, household documents, photos and the operation history are sent to Apple CloudKit, in private or shared databases. Technical household, iCloud account and operation identifiers are stored to synchronise data and control access. Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply. We do not promise end-to-end encryption or technically impossible developer access.

Shared households and photos

People invited to a household can read its data, including comments, ratings and photos; people with write permission can edit it. Members recorded for calculations are different from iCloud participants. Only share information you are entitled to share and inform the people concerned.

Apple’s picker provides the images you select. The app creates a smaller JPEG copy that omits personal metadata from the source; visible content may still show people or other personal information. It does not change or delete the original in your photo library.

Reminders and recipients

Local reminders are optional and require iOS permission. Product or equipment names may appear on your lock screen; you can manage previews in system settings. CloudKit uses Apple notification services to update synchronisation.

HomeRunway has no backend of its own. Apple provides the iCloud services you choose to use, and authorised participants receive shared content. If you export or send a file, your chosen destination receives it.

Support is handled at inventsweneed@gmail.com using Google’s personal Gmail service. The owner receives your email address, message content and any files you choose to send. Google processes this information to provide its service under its privacy policy. Do not send passwords, sensitive information or backups containing personal data.

Purposes and legal bases

The owner is responsible for the data received when you contact support. It is used to reply and resolve issues, based on the legitimate interest in handling voluntary enquiries and maintaining the app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), and to comply with applicable legal obligations, including rights requests (Article 6(1)(c)). Provide only the information needed.

Household management takes place on your device and, if you enable iCloud, through Apple services under your account. You decide the content and the people you share it with. The app does not send a copy of the inventory to the owner or use that content for advertising, tracking or its own analytics. Apple processes data in its services under its terms and privacy policy. Purely personal or household use may fall outside the GDPR; other uses require their own assessment.

Retention, export and deletion

Data remains while you keep it. Archiving products or locations and deactivating members preserves records and history. Removing a photo, comment or member from the current view does not necessarily purge previous versions from the local journal, synchronisation documents or copies.

You can export a JSON copy from Backup and restore. The file is not encrypted and may include photos, information about people and history. Once exported it is in your custody: store it securely, limit sharing and remove copies you no longer need. Deleting data in the app does not delete that file or copies you have sent to others. Recoveries of pending changes are independent copies.

Deleting a local-only household removes its local records. Deleting your own iCloud household or leaving a shared one requires a connection and an access check. The owner deletes the household’s cloud zone. Using the delete/leave action in Households removes your access and the local copy on that device, but not the owner’s data. Leaving through Apple’s panel or losing access may retain a local recovery copy. Other devices, recoveries, exports and copies held by others may retain data requiring explicit deletion. Uninstalling the app does not itself remove iCloud data or exported files. Immediate remote erasure is not guaranteed.

Support enquiries are kept while they are being handled, and the owner deletes the messages and their own copies no later than 12 months after closure. They may be deleted earlier when no longer needed or where an erasure request should be granted. This period does not justify retaining unnecessary information. Deletion from the mailbox does not guarantee the immediate disappearance of all Google technical copies; Google applies its own retention and deletion processes.

Your rights

For data processed by the owner, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability where applicable, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it, by writing to inventsweneed@gmail.com. Requests will be answered within the applicable GDPR deadline (generally one month, subject to lawful extensions). You can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD): www.aepd.es.

Manage local records in the app and Apple services through your account. A request to the owner cannot erase other participants’ copies or files outside the owner’s control. Do not send passwords or identity documents unless necessary for a specific request.

Information website

The mini website adds no cookies, analytics, ads, forms or tracking resources. When published on GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security and provides hosting under its privacy statement. Opening the site connects to that provider; these texts can also be read offline in the app.

GitHub describes processing in several countries, including the United States, and international transfer mechanisms in its privacy statement. Apple describes its services and transfers in its privacy policy. Google may also process support messages outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States; according to its published information, it uses adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses where applicable. See its privacy policy and transfer frameworks for the scope of those safeguards.
