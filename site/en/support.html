Support

Contact

Write to inventsweneed@gmail.com. Include the version and build (Settings), device, iOS/iPadOS version, steps to reproduce the issue and what you expected. Say whether the household is local, iCloud or shared; use fictional names. No response time is promised.

Report a bug safely

Do not send passwords, Apple codes, invitation links, certificates or backups containing personal information. Crop or anonymise screenshots. JSON backups are not encrypted and include household content; create a test household with fictional data to reproduce an issue.

Does it work offline?

Local management works offline. Synchronising, accepting invitations and deleting or leaving iCloud households requires a connection. Pending changes may wait until access returns; check iCloud status before deleting or restoring.

Why do estimates differ?

They depend on quantities, units, consumption and configured needs. Incomplete data may produce partial coverage or no estimate. Energy is shown as capacity in Wh; it does not represent days of autonomy.

Is my comment or photo shared?

Yes, it belongs to the household and can be seen by anyone with access to the shared household. iCloud participants and members used in calculations are different concepts.

How do I export or delete data?

Use Backup and restore to export. Archiving or deactivating preserves data; removing a current annotation may retain previous versions. Deleting your own iCloud household requires a connection; leaving a shared one does not delete the owner’s household. Also review your exports and copies.

How do I turn reminders off?

In Settings → Notifications you can disable reminders or their categories. If iOS denied permission, change permission in system settings. Lock screen previews are also controlled in iOS.
